Privacy Policy

Content Factory Drive · Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Content Factory Drive application ("the App") handles information when you connect your Google Drive account to it. The App is operated by Anton Bychkovsky ("we", "us", or "our") as a private productivity tool used to upload, organize, and edit documents in your own Google Drive.

2. Information the App Accesses

The App requests only the minimum scopes required to operate:

  • drive.file — to create, read, and modify files that you explicitly choose to upload or edit via the App.
  • documents — to export and re-upload Google Docs in standard formats.

The App does not request access to:

  • Your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or Google Drive files you have not explicitly shared with the App.
  • Your Google account password or authentication credentials beyond the OAuth token issued by Google.
  • Any data outside the scope described above.

3. How We Use the Data

The App uses the data it accesses solely to:

  • Upload files you provide to your Google Drive.
  • Convert files between formats (for example, between text and Google Docs).
  • Display file metadata (name, size, modified date) back to you.

We do not analyze, summarize, train, or sell your files. We do not share them with any third party.

4. Where the Data Is Stored

All files accessed by the App remain in your own Google Drive. We do not copy files to any external server. The App runs locally on the operator's machine and interacts with Google Drive over Google's official APIs.

5. Data Retention

We retain no copies of your files. The OAuth refresh token is stored locally on the operator's machine in .secrets/google_drive/token.json and is used only to make authorized API calls on your behalf. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

6. Sharing

We do not share your data with any third party. The App has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no advertising partners. The App is operated as a private single-operator tool.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
